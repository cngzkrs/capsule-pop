Capsule Pop – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Capsule Pop ("the game") is made by an independent developer based in Çanakkale, Türkiye ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or any other contact details.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your progress (levels, toys, coins, boosters, lives and Collector Pass progress), your settings (sound, music, vibration, colour symbols, language) and the purchases you made are saved on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example "Continue" or "+1 life") and, from level 20, occasional full-screen ads between levels. Players who bought "No Ads" or made any purchase do not see full-screen ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for permission before the IDFA is used. If you decline, ads are not personalised with it.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

Optional purchases ("No Ads", bundles, coin packs, the Gold Pass) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the store only tells the game that the purchase was made so it can be delivered and, where possible, restored.

The game has no account or login: everything you get in the game is kept only on the device it was installed on. Coins, boosters, lives, the Collector Pass (Gold Pass) and the rest of your progress, including what you bought with real money, are deleted for good if you uninstall the game, reset or replace your device, or clear the app's data. We cannot see or restore them, and they are not refunded.

"No Ads" is the exception: it is a permanent purchase linked to your Apple ID or Google account. After reinstalling, or on a new device signed in with the same account, tap Settings > Restore purchases and it comes back for free. The coins and boosters that came with a bundle are not restored.

4. Notifications

If you allow them, reminders (your next toy, your lives are full) are scheduled on your device as local notifications, never at night. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in your device settings.

5. Analytics

This version of the game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services. If we add them (for example Google Firebase), we will update this policy before the version that includes them is released.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to its processing. The game keeps your game data only on your device. For advertising data you can reset your advertising ID, change your consent in Settings > Privacy, or contact us and we will forward your request to Google.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
